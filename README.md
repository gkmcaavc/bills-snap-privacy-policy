# 🧾 Bills Snap – Privacy Policy

*Last Updated: April 2026*

Welcome to **Bills Snap**.
Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how your information is collected, used, and protected when using the Bills Snap application.

---

# 📌 About Bills Snap

Bills Snap is an offline-first expense tracker and bill scanner application that helps users:

* 📸 Scan bills using OCR or AI
* 💰 Track expenses
* 📊 View analytics and budgets
* 🔔 Manage reminders
* 📦 Backup and restore data

---

# 📂 Information We Collect

## 📷 Bill Images

When you scan or upload bills, the app may process:

* Bill images
* OCR text
* Extracted bill information

---

## 🧾 Expense Information

The app stores:

* Shop names
* Items and prices
* Categories
* Payment methods
* Bill totals
* Reminder and budget information

---

## ⚙️ App Settings

The app may store:

* Currency selection
* Theme settings
* Custom categories
* AI provider selection

---

# 🤖 AI Processing

If AI scanning is enabled:

* Bill data may be processed using:

  * Claude
  * ChatGPT
  * Gemini

AI processing depends on:

* Your selected provider
* Your API key configuration

---

# 🔐 API Keys

Your AI API keys:

* Are stored locally on your device
* Are not shared with us

# 💾 Data Storage

Bills Snap is designed as an **offline-first application**.

✅ Your data is primarily stored locally on your device.

We do **not** sell or share your personal data.
